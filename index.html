About CarePath

A simple starting point for coverage support

CarePath is a prototype intake tool for care teams to note when a patient may want help exploring medical insurance coverage and arranging a follow-up.

1. Record the basics

Enter a patient's contact details, current coverage status, and whether they would like assistance. Notes should be brief and avoid sensitive medical or insurance account details.

2. Review the session list

New entries appear in the patient list. Search by name, review the coverage and follow-up summary, or remove an entry. The summary numbers update as records change.

3. Arrange a follow-up

The form helps a care team identify who asked for support. It does not contact patients or connect them with an insurer, broker, or enrollment service.

Important: This demonstration stores entries only in the current page session. Refreshing or closing the tab clears them. It does not save or send records, determine eligibility, enroll anyone in a plan, or replace guidance from a qualified insurance assister. Do not use it with real patient information.
